About the Project

This application is a messaging service that will be used by students and teachers to communicate with each other. Users can join public group chats, chat with other users privately or publicly, and view the online status of other users. Through this app, students can discuss homework, teachers can make announcements to the class, and all users can receive important news about the school, such as school events, delays, or schedule changes.
